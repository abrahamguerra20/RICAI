The current scientific publishing crisis

Exorbitant APC costs

Traditional open-access models rely heavily on Article Processing Charges (APCs) that force investigators to spend thousands of dollars to publish their outputs, systematically excluding independent researchers and regional institutions.

Knowledge gatekeeping

Commercial publishing networks profit off public-funded research, locking rigorous findings behind premium paywalls or high subscription costs, restricting the universal flow of peer-reviewed data.

Uncompensated peer review

The evaluation infrastructure depends entirely on the uncompensated intellectual hours of academic reviewers, while centralized corporate publishing frameworks retain full financial and structural control.

Upcoming Initiative

A true Diamond Open Access platform

To resolve these systemic barriers, RICAI is developing its own independent editorial framework. This upcoming journal will operate under a strict Diamond Open Access model, meaning it will be completely free of charge for both authors and readers. By eliminating processing fees and institutional gatekeeping, the platform aims to establish a peer-reviewed repository where methodological rigor and scientific clarity are the sole criteria for publication.
