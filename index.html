Our Leadership

Abraham David Guerra Ospino

CEO & Founder
Abraham is a Microbiologist and Bioinformatician, and a Master’s candidate in the Genetics and Plant Breeding program at São Paulo State University (UNESP), Brazil. He is currently conducting an international research exchange at the University of Padua (UNIPD), Italy.

His research specializes in pangenomics and the study of complex microbial ecosystems, focusing on the genomic characterization of the genus Utricularia and its associated microbiome. He is a strong advocate for Open Science and the development of reproducible research workflows.
🇧🇷

Nilo Corrêa de Mello Júnior Ricardo

Lead Researcher
Nilo is an Agronomist and a PhD candidate in Plant Production at UNESP, Brazil. He holds a Master’s degree in Irrigated Horticulture and has specialized in Data Science and Food Technology, combining expertise in agriculture and data-driven approaches.

His work focuses on postharvest physiology, using Near-Infrared (NIR) spectroscopy and machine learning to predict fruit quality and optimal harvest timing. He develops interdisciplinary approaches that link agriculture, food science, and human nutrition to support more efficient and sustainable production systems.
