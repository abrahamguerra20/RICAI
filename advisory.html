Why Advisory

Navigating academia requires more than talent

Getting into a top PhD program, securing an international fellowship, or publishing in a high-impact journal is not just about the quality of your science. It requires knowing how to present yourself, who to contact, how to write a compelling statement of purpose, and dozens of other skills that elite institutions teach informally — and that most researchers from underrepresented regions never access.

RICAI's advisory services exist to close that gap. Our team has navigated these processes across Colombia, Brazil, Italy, the United States, and beyond — and we offer that experience directly to our members.

International Trajectory

Our advisors have navigated academic systems across 10+ countries — from scholarship applications to international exchanges.

Peer-to-Peer Model

Guidance from researchers who recently went through the same processes — not generic advice, but real, current experience.

Free for Network Members

All advisory pathways are available to RICAI members at no cost — consistent with our commitment to career equity.
