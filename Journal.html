The Problem

The current scientific publishing crisis

Academic publishing is a multi-billion dollar industry built on publicly funded research — where scientists write for free, review for free, and then pay to access the results of their own work. RICAI exists to build an alternative.

Exorbitant Article Processing Charges

Traditional open-access models rely on APCs that force investigators to spend $1,500–$12,000 per article to publish their outputs. This systematically excludes independent researchers, early-career scientists, and institutions in the Global South — the exact communities RICAI serves.

Avg. APC in top journals: $3,000–$12,000

Knowledge Locked Behind Paywalls

Commercial publishing networks profit from public-funded research by locking peer-reviewed findings behind premium subscription walls. A researcher in Latin America or Sub-Saharan Africa often cannot access the very literature their field is built on — unless their institution pays millions per year in subscription fees.

70%+ of researchers lack full journal access

Uncompensated Peer Review

The entire evaluation infrastructure of science depends on the uncompensated intellectual labor of academic reviewers — who read, critique, and validate manuscripts for free — while commercial publishers retain full financial control and charge both sides of the transaction.

15M+ hours of unpaid review work annually
The Solution

A true Diamond Open Access platform

Upcoming Initiative

The RICAI Journal — built different

To resolve these systemic barriers, RICAI is developing its own independent editorial framework. This upcoming journal will operate under a strict Diamond Open Access model — meaning it will be completely free of charge for both authors and readers, forever.

By eliminating processing fees and institutional gatekeeping, the RICAI Journal aims to establish a peer-reviewed repository where methodological rigor and scientific clarity are the sole criteria for publication — not your institution's prestige or your access to funding.

Zero Article Processing Charges

Authors never pay to publish. No submission fees, no APC, no hidden costs — ever.

Zero Reader Paywalls

Every article is immediately and permanently free to read, download, and share worldwide.

Transparent Peer Review

Open review process where methodology and scientific clarity — not prestige — determine publication.

Decentralized Editorial Board

International reviewers from diverse institutional backgrounds — no single institution controls the process.

Editorial Philosophy

What the RICAI Journal stands for

These principles are non-negotiable — they define what makes the RICAI Journal different from every commercial alternative.

Merit-Only Publication

Articles are accepted or rejected based exclusively on methodological rigor, scientific clarity, and contribution to knowledge. Institutional affiliation, funding source, and author prestige play no role in editorial decisions.

Global South Priority

We actively seek research from underrepresented regions. The scientific community has a geographic bias problem — we treat diversity of research origin as a quality indicator, not an afterthought.

Reproducibility & Open Data

All published research must adhere to open data and reproducibility standards. Code, datasets, and methodological protocols are published alongside articles whenever possible.

Independence from Commercial Interests

No advertising, no sponsored content, no influence from pharmaceutical companies, agribusiness, or any commercial entity. Editorial independence is structural, not a policy — it is built into how the journal is funded and governed.
