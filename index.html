What is RICAI?

RICAI is a collaborative research network designed to break down the barriers of traditional academic institutions. We are a central hub where young talent connects with experts to produce high-impact science, leveraging open-access data to democratize knowledge.



Independent Research

Conduct original research using global open-access databases, mentored by experienced scientists.

Global Career

Open Science

We believe knowledge should be accessible. We publish, share, and collaborate without borders.
